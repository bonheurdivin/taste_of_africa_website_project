Jollof Rice

A beloved West African classic, rich in colour, flavour, and cultural tradition.

PREPARATION 20 minutes
COOKING 50 minutes
TOTAL TIME 1 hour 10 minutes
DIFFICULTY Easy

A TASTE OF NIGERIA

A Dish That Brings People Together

Jollof Rice is one of West Africa's most celebrated dishes. Its signature red-orange colour comes from a rich tomato and pepper base, while aromatic seasonings give it its distinctive flavour.

Across West Africa, different countries prepare their own versions of Jollof Rice. In Nigeria, it is especially popular at family gatherings, celebrations, parties, and everyday meals.
