Moroccan Chicken Tagine

A slow-cooked Moroccan classic, rich with warming spices, tender chicken, preserved lemons and green olives.

Preparation 20 minutes
Cooking 1 hour
Total time 1 hour 20 minutes
Difficulty Medium

A taste of Morocco

A tradition built around slow cooking

Tagine is one of the most recognised dishes of Moroccan cuisine. Its name refers both to the meal and the traditional earthenware cooking vessel with a distinctive cone-shaped lid.

Across Morocco, tagines bring together local ingredients and fragrant spices. This chicken version combines ginger, turmeric, saffron, preserved lemons and olives to create a beautiful balance of savoury, aromatic and tangy flavours.

Let's get cooking

Cooking instructions

1. Prepare the chicken

Place the chicken in a bowl. Add the garlic, ginger, turmeric, cumin, paprika, black pepper, saffron, olive oil and a little salt. Mix well and leave to marinate for at least 30 minutes in the refrigerator.

2. Prepare the onion base

Heat a tagine or heavy-bottomed pot over medium-low heat. Add the sliced onions and cook gently until they begin to soften.

3. Add the chicken

Place the marinated chicken into the pot. Turn the pieces so they are coated with the onion and spice mixture.

4. Slow-cook the tagine

Pour in the chicken stock or water. Cover and simmer gently for approximately 45â€“50 minutes, turning the chicken occasionally, until it is thoroughly cooked and tender.

5. Add lemons and olives

Add the preserved lemon and green olives. Continue cooking for approximately 10â€“15 minutes so their flavours blend into the sauce.

6. Garnish and serve

Sprinkle with freshly chopped coriander and parsley. Serve warm with Moroccan bread or couscous.
