A continent of flavours

Explore Africa, one region at a time.

From the rich stews of West Africa to the aromatic spices of North Africa and the diverse traditions of the East, discover the stories behind Africa's food.

Discover our food heritage

Three regions. Countless flavours.

African cuisine is shaped by geography, history, local ingredients and generations of culinary knowledge. Explore some of its fascinating regional traditions below.

01. West Africa

Bold flavours and vibrant traditions

Discover a cuisine known for its richly seasoned rice dishes, hearty soups, grilled foods and creative use of local ingredients.

Popular staples: Rice, cassava, yam and millet.

Discover West Africa

02. East Africa

Heritage, hospitality and diversity

Explore a diverse food culture featuring fermented breads, plantains, stews, grains and dishes shaped by local traditions and regional influences.

Popular staples: Maize, teff, cassava, bananas and beans.

Discover East Africa

03. North Africa

Ancient influences and aromatic spices

Experience a culinary landscape shaped by Mediterranean, Amazigh and Arab traditions, with fragrant spices, slow-cooked dishes and breads.

Popular staples: Couscous, wheat, olives and chickpeas.

Discover North Africa

Beyond the plate

Food connects generations.

Across Africa, recipes are more than instructions. They carry memories, celebrate communities and preserve knowledge passed from one generation to another.
